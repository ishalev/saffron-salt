# Version A: Isaac solo

Draft 3, 2026-11-16. Isaac's edits. No event-specific references, so the video stays usable after PAX Unplugged. About 420 spoken words, roughly 2:45.

## 1. Hello (0:00–0:25)
*Picture: title card, then the printed prototype on a table.*

Hi, I'm Isaac Shalev, designer of *Tír na nÓg*. I made this game with Mitch Wallace, the designer of *Art Society*. It's called **Saffron Salt** and it features fresh spices, bold bids, salty rivals. It's an auction game for two to five players, in thirty to forty-five minutes.

In Saffron Salt you play as rival spice traders, competing to supply a great food emporium - think WholeFoods, but not trademark infringing. Over three seasons, you buy spices at auction to win the emporium's business, and whoever finishes with the most points wins.

## 2. The auction (0:25–1:00)
*Picture: the digital market of lots; bids revealed; players choosing lots in order.*

Each auction, the market has one lot of spice cards per player. Everyone secretly bids a single card, or a pair of the same spice. Any pair beats a single; otherwise the higher card wins. Then, from the highest bid down, each player takes one lot of spices.

Here's the twist: the cards you bid don't disappear. They become part of the next lots in the market. So every bid asks three questions: what do I want, how badly do I want it, and what am I willing to put into the market?

## 3. The tracks (1:00–1:50)
*Picture: the seven spice tracks with the minimum-order line highlighted; markers climbing; a skip over a crowded track; a salted card blocked; a knock-down from the top.*

Each of the seven spice tracks is your standing with the emporium for that spice. The emporium will buy from any trader who can meet its minimum order. But it buys the most from its preferred supplier: the one who can bring it the most spice, and the freshest.

Every spice card in your lot moves you up that spice's track. Occupied spaces get skipped, so crowded tracks race fast. But salted cards can't skip. If someone's right in front of you, you're stuck - you've been caught padding your spice sacks with salt!

Reach the top and you get paid on the spot. And if someone's already up there, you knock them back down to the minimum order. Timing matters! Get ahead of your opponents and move up before they can climb over you.

You also keep one card from every lot you buy. It comes back to your hand next season.

## 4. Scoring (1:50–2:25)
*Picture: end-of-season storage moves, then the track scoring; final cash-out and specialization.*

At the end of each season, the cards left in your hand move you up the tracks, too. Then the emporium buys, track by track: its preferred supplier, the trader leading the track, earns the big bonus, and every other trader who made the minimum order earns something too.

After three seasons, your saved cards make one last bid for a cash payout. And there's a bonus for every track you never touched, so specializing is a real strategy.

## 5. Close (2:25–2:40)
*Picture: closing card with the title, the link and both names.*

Saffron Salt is quick to teach, it plays fast, and every bid is a tough call. We hope to show it to you at an upcoming pitch meeting. Or you can play it right now, against bots or friends, at kindfortress.com/saffron-salt. Thanks for watching!
